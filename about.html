ABOUT ICTAZ CBU

Building a stronger ICT community.

ICTAZ CBU brings students together to learn, build practical skills and connect with the wider ICT community.

WHAT IS ICTAZ?

Supporting the growth of ICT professionals in Zambia.

ICTAZ is the Information and Communication Technology Association of Zambia. It is a professional body that supports the development and growth of the ICT profession in Zambia.

For students, ICTAZ provides an opportunity to begin connecting with the wider ICT community while developing practical and professional skills.
