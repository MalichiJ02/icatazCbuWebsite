ABOUT ICTAZ CBU

Building a stronger ICT community at CBU.

ICTAZ CBU brings students together around technology, practical learning, collaboration and professional development.

WHAT IS ICTAZ?

Connecting students to the wider ICT profession.

ICTAZ is the Information and Communication Technology Association of Zambia, a professional body supporting the development and growth of the ICT profession in Zambia.

For students, engagement with ICTAZ creates an opportunity to begin developing professional connections, practical experience and awareness of the wider ICT industry.

CBU

ICTAZ CBU

A student technology community at CBU Lusaka Campus focused on learning, building, connecting, contributing and developing future leaders.

WHO WE ARE

A community built around students.

ICTAZ CBU exists to create an active environment where students interested in technology can grow beyond what they learn in lectures.

The goal is not simply to organise events. We want students to practise skills, build projects, collaborate, discover opportunities and develop the confidence to contribute to the ICT community.

OUR MISSION

Turn learning into experience.

To create opportunities for students to develop practical skills, work together, build meaningful projects and connect with the wider ICT community.

OUR VISION

Build an active and sustainable student community.

A CBU technology community that develops skilled, connected and confident students prepared to contribute to Zambia's ICT sector.

OUR DIRECTION

From learning to leadership.

Our direction follows five stages that describe how students can grow through the community.

01

Learn

Develop practical technology and professional skills beyond the classroom.

02

Build

Apply those skills through projects, challenges and collaborative work.

03

Connect

Build relationships with students, professionals, mentors and industry.

04

Contribute

Use your knowledge, ideas and skills to support the community and solve problems.

05

Lead

Develop the confidence and experience to organise, mentor and lead others.
