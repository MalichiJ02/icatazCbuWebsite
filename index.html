WHO WE ARE

Technology doesn't end in the classroom.

ICTAZ CBU brings students together around technology, practical learning, collaboration and professional development at CBU Lusaka Campus.

We are building a community where students can learn from one another, work on real ideas, develop their skills and connect with the wider ICT community.

OUR DIRECTION

From learning to leadership.

ICTAZ CBU is built around a simple journey: learn something, build something, connect with others, contribute and eventually lead.

01

Learn

Develop practical technology and professional skills through workshops, peer learning and hands-on sessions.

02

Build

Apply what you know through projects, challenges and collaborative problem solving.

03

Connect

Meet students, mentors, professionals and people across the wider ICT community.

04

Contribute

Use your skills and ideas to support projects, communities and initiatives that matter.

05

Lead

Grow into someone capable of leading teams, organising initiatives and helping others grow.
